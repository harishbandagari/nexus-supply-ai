SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTION ALERT — SHENZHEN MANUFACTURING LOCKDOWN
Alert ID: DISRUPT-2026-001
Date Issued: May 15, 2026
Severity: CRITICAL
Region: Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, China
Status: Active

SUMMARY
A government-mandated operational restriction has been imposed on manufacturing facilities in the Shenzhen Special Economic Zone effective May 12, 2026. The restriction impacts all electronics manufacturing operations in the Shenzhen-Bao'an and Shenzhen-Longhua industrial districts. Estimated duration: 21–45 days.

IMPACTED FACILITIES
The following supplier facilities are confirmed impacted:
1. Shenzhen FlexCircuit Co. (SUP-001) — Flexible Printed Circuits manufacturing facility in Bao'an District. Full production halt confirmed.
2. ShenTech Actuator Systems (SUP-003) — Camera Actuator Module assembly facility in Longhua District. Full production halt confirmed.
3. Shenzhen Optoelectronics (SUP-021) — OLED Driver IC packaging and test facility in Nanshan District. Partial operations, running at 30% capacity.

IMPACTED COMPONENTS
- Flexible Printed Circuits (FPC) — Critical supply disruption. SUP-001 accounts for 35% of FPC sourcing allocation.
- Camera Actuator Modules — Critical supply disruption. SUP-003 accounts for 40% of Camera Actuator sourcing.
- OLED Driver ICs — Partial disruption. SUP-021 running at reduced capacity, but SUP-002 (Guangzhou) currently unaffected.

SUPPLY IMPACT ASSESSMENT
- FPC: Current pipeline inventory covers approximately 18 days of production demand. After pipeline depletion, production line stoppage risk is HIGH.
- Camera Actuators: Pipeline inventory covers 14 days. Production impact is CRITICAL given limited alternate sources.
- OLED Driver ICs: SUP-021 reduced capacity offset partially by SUP-002 and SUP-017 availability. Impact is MEDIUM.

LOGISTICS IMPACT
- Shenzhen port operations are functioning but experiencing 3-5 day delays due to reduced trucking capacity
- Air freight from Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport operating at 60% capacity
- Cross-border trucking to Hong Kong experiencing 2-day delays

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
1. Activate Business Continuity Plans for SUP-001 and SUP-003 per contract terms
2. Assess alternate supplier capacity: Malaysia Circuit Assembly (SUP-007) for FPC, Nagoya Actuator Corp (SUP-023) for Camera Actuators
3. Evaluate emergency sourcing from Non-AVL suppliers if AVL alternates insufficient
4. Increase safety stock targets for impacted components by 50%
5. Notify downstream manufacturing partners of potential 2-4 week supply delays
6. Schedule daily supply chain situation room meetings
