SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTION ALERT — TARIFF INCREASE ON CHINESE ELECTRONICS IMPORTS
Alert ID: DISRUPT-2026-004
Date Issued: May 25, 2026
Severity: MEDIUM
Region: United States (import tariffs on Chinese-origin goods)
Status: Pending (effective July 1, 2026)

SUMMARY
The U.S. Trade Representative has announced an increase in Section 301 tariffs on Chinese-origin electronics components from 25% to 35%, effective July 1, 2026. The tariff increase applies to HTS categories covering printed circuits, semiconductor components, display components, battery cells, and passive electronic components.

AFFECTED COMPONENT CATEGORIES
- Flexible Printed Circuits (HTS 8534.00) — 35% tariff (up from 25%)
- OLED Driver ICs (HTS 8542.31) — 35% tariff (up from 25%)
- Camera Actuator Modules (HTS 8501.10) — 35% tariff (up from 25%)
- NAND Flash Memory (HTS 8542.32) — 35% tariff (up from 25%)
- Battery Cells (HTS 8507.60) — 35% tariff (up from 25%)
- MLCC Capacitors (HTS 8532.24) — 35% tariff (up from 25%)
- Semiconductor Substrates (HTS 3818.00) — 35% tariff (up from 25%)

IMPACTED SUPPLIERS (CHINESE ORIGIN)
- SUP-001: Shenzhen FlexCircuit Co. (FPC)
- SUP-002: Guangzhou Display Tech (OLED Driver ICs)
- SUP-003: ShenTech Actuator Systems (Camera Actuators)

FINANCIAL IMPACT ESTIMATE
Based on trailing 12-month procurement volumes from Chinese suppliers:
- Additional annual tariff cost: estimated $8.2M - $12.5M
- Per-unit cost increase: 7-10% across affected categories
- Landed cost competitiveness of Chinese suppliers significantly reduced versus non-China alternatives

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
1. Model total landed cost comparison: Chinese suppliers (with new tariff) vs. non-China AVL alternatives
2. Accelerate sourcing qualification for non-China suppliers in affected categories
3. Evaluate tariff engineering opportunities (first sale valuation, FTZ utilization)
4. Negotiate cost-sharing arrangements with affected Chinese suppliers
5. Review contracts for tariff pass-through clauses and obligations
6. Pre-ship maximum inventory before July 1 effective date to lock in current tariff rates
7. Update procurement analytics dashboard with tariff-adjusted landed costs
