INCIDENT REPORT — KOREAN OLED PANEL FAB FIRE DISRUPTS FOLDABLE DISPLAY SUPPLY
Incident ID: INC-2026-OLED-KR-003
Reported: February 12, 2026
Severity: CRITICAL
Affected Component Category: Display Components — Foldable OLED Panels, OLED Driver ICs
Geographic Region: Cheongju, South Korea

SUMMARY
A fire in the deposition module of SUP-051's (Cheongju Flexible OLED) Generation 6 flexible OLED fabrication facility on February 9, 2026, has resulted in significant equipment damage. Initial assessments indicate the affected deposition chamber is offline for an estimated 12-16 weeks. SUP-051 has issued a force majeure notification covering all foldable OLED panel deliveries.

OPERATIONAL IMPACT
- SUP-051 foldable OLED panel output reduced by 60% for the next 90-120 days
- Knock-on effect on SUP-002 (Guangzhou Display Tech) OLED driver IC demand reallocation
- Foldable smartphone NPI ramps for upcoming flagship launch at high risk
- No qualified Tier 1 alternative for Buyer's current foldable display specification

DEPENDENT PRODUCT IMPACT
Foldable flagship smartphone product line — projected Q3 launch — may slip 8-12 weeks if alternative supply cannot be qualified.

MITIGATION ACTIONS REQUIRED
1. Engage SUP-052 (Hefei Foldable Display) for emergency qualification — Tier 2, AVL, but reliability score 0.85 and risk score 0.62 (geographic concentration concern)
2. Negotiate temporary allocation increase from SUP-005 (Korea Display Corp) for rigid OLED on non-foldable product lines to free capacity elsewhere
3. Review feasibility of a one-product-cycle delay vs. specification downgrade for the foldable launch
4. Initiate dual-source qualification program for foldable OLED — current single-source posture is a critical strategic vulnerability

RISK CLASSIFICATION
- Single-source risk: CRITICAL — foldable OLED at flagship specification has no dual source
- Recovery timeline: 12-16 weeks
- Recurrence probability: Low for fire specifically, but underlying single-source concentration risk is structural
