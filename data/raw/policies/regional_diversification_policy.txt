ENTERPRISE PROCUREMENT POLICY — REGIONAL SOURCING DIVERSIFICATION
Policy ID: PROC-POL-002
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: March 1, 2024
Owner: SVP Supply Chain Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy mandates regional diversification of sourcing to mitigate concentration risk in critical hardware component categories. Excessive reliance on a single geographic region exposes the organization to disruption risks including geopolitical instability, natural disasters, trade restrictions, and logistics bottlenecks.

2. REGIONAL CONCENTRATION LIMITS
For each critical component category, the following regional sourcing concentration limits apply:

(a) No single country shall account for more than 60% of sourcing volume for any critical component category
(b) No single geographic corridor (e.g., Shenzhen-Dongguan, Yangtze River Delta) shall exceed 45% of category volume
(c) Minimum of 2 distinct geographic regions must be represented in the AVL for Tier 1 components
(d) Minimum of 3 distinct countries must be represented for annual procurement spend exceeding $50M per category

3. CRITICAL COMPONENT CATEGORIES
The following categories are designated as critical and subject to mandatory diversification:
- Display Components (OLED Panels, Driver ICs, Display Glass)
- Semiconductor Materials (Wafers, Substrates)
- Interconnect (Flexible Printed Circuits, Connectors)
- Camera Components (Actuator Modules, Lens Assemblies)
- Power Components (Battery Cells, PMICs)
- Memory Components (NAND Flash, DRAM)
- Passive Components (MLCCs, Resistors)
- RF Components (Front-End Modules, Antennas)

4. DIVERSIFICATION REVIEW TRIGGERS
Automatic sourcing diversification review is triggered when:
(a) Regional sourcing concentration exceeds limits defined in Section 2
(b) A regional disruption event impacts supplier operations for 14+ consecutive days
(c) New trade restrictions, tariffs, or export controls are imposed on a sourcing region
(d) A Tier 1 supplier in a concentrated region experiences AVL status change
(e) Annual strategic sourcing review identifies emerging concentration risk

5. DIVERSIFICATION ACTIONS
When concentration limits are exceeded, the procurement team must:
(a) Identify alternate suppliers in different geographic regions within 30 days
(b) Initiate qualification process for at least one alternate supplier within 60 days
(c) Achieve compliant diversification within 180 days
(d) Report diversification progress to the Supply Chain Risk Committee monthly

6. EXCEPTIONS
Exceptions to regional concentration limits require:
- Written justification from Category Manager
- Risk assessment by Supply Chain Risk Committee
- SVP Supply Chain Operations approval
- Quarterly exception review and renewal
Exceptions are granted for a maximum of 12 months.

7. MONITORING AND REPORTING
Regional sourcing concentration is monitored monthly via the Procurement Analytics Dashboard. Category Managers are responsible for maintaining compliance. Quarterly compliance reports are submitted to the Supply Chain Risk Committee.
