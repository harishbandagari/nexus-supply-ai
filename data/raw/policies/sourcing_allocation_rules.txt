ENTERPRISE PROCUREMENT POLICY — SOURCING ALLOCATION RULES
Policy ID: PROC-POL-003
Version: 1.4
Effective Date: February 1, 2024
Owner: Director of Strategic Sourcing

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines sourcing allocation rules governing the distribution of procurement volume across approved suppliers within each component category. Allocation caps ensure supply continuity, prevent over-reliance on individual suppliers, and maintain competitive sourcing leverage.

2. ALLOCATION CAP ENFORCEMENT
(a) Each supplier's sourcing allocation cap is defined in the Master Supply Agreement and recorded in the Supplier Registry
(b) Allocation caps are expressed as a percentage of total category procurement volume
(c) Allocation caps must be validated before any purchase order exceeding $100K or 10,000 units
(d) Systems must block purchase orders that would cause a supplier's trailing 12-month allocation to exceed their cap

3. ALLOCATION ADJUSTMENT PROCESS
Allocation cap adjustments require:
(a) Category Manager recommendation with business justification
(b) Supply Chain Risk assessment
(c) VP Procurement approval for adjustments up to 10 percentage points
(d) SVP Supply Chain Operations approval for adjustments exceeding 10 percentage points
(e) 90-day implementation lead time for allocation increases
(f) 30-day notice to affected suppliers for allocation decreases

4. EMERGENCY ALLOCATION OVERRIDES
During declared supply disruptions, temporary allocation overrides are permitted:
(a) VP Procurement may authorize temporary override for up to 30 days
(b) Override must not exceed 150% of the supplier's standard allocation cap
(c) Override documentation must include: disruption event, expected duration, risk assessment, and reversion plan
(d) All overrides are logged in the Procurement Audit System

5. ALLOCATION MONITORING
Category Managers monitor allocation compliance monthly. The Procurement Analytics system generates alerts when:
(a) Any supplier reaches 80% of their allocation cap (warning)
(b) Any supplier reaches 95% of their allocation cap (critical alert)
(c) Any supplier exceeds their allocation cap (compliance violation)
Compliance violations require corrective action within 30 days.
