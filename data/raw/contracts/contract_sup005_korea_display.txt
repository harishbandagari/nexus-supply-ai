MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT — KOREA DISPLAY CORP (SUP-005)
Contract Reference: MSA-2024-OLED-005
Effective Date: June 1, 2024
Expiration Date: September 30, 2027
Contracting Party: Global Hardware Manufacturing Corp.

ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE OF SUPPLY
Korea Display Corp ("Supplier") shall manufacture and deliver OLED Panel assemblies for premium smartphone and tablet product lines. Scope includes LTPO OLED panels, flexible OLED panels, and under-display integration modules as specified in Exhibit E.

ARTICLE 2 — MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY
Minimum order quantity is 2,000 panel units per purchase order. Quarterly commitment: 20,000 units minimum. Panel customization orders (curved, foldable) carry a minimum of 5,000 units and 45-day lead time.

ARTICLE 3 — SOURCING ALLOCATION
Maximum allocation: 35% of total OLED Panel procurement. Supplier is designated as a strategic dual-source partner alongside Kaohsiung Display Systems (SUP-022). Combined allocation from both OLED Panel suppliers shall not exceed 55% from any single country.

ARTICLE 4 — LEAD TIME AND DELIVERY
Standard lead time: 30 calendar days for flat OLED panels. Flexible OLED: 38 calendar days. Foldable OLED: 45 calendar days. On-time delivery target: 97%.

ARTICLE 5 — HEADROOM CAPACITY
Supplier guarantees 75,000 units headroom capacity across all panel types. Capacity is allocated: 60% flat OLED, 25% flexible OLED, 15% foldable OLED. Reallocation between panel types requires 21-day notice.

ARTICLE 6 — FORCE MAJEURE
Standard provisions apply. Supplier's Seoul facility is assessed as low geopolitical risk. However, in the event of Korean Peninsula security escalation, Supplier must activate its Cheonan backup facility within 7 days and provide Buyer with revised production timelines within 72 hours.

ARTICLE 7 — QUALITY AND YIELD
Panel yield rate minimum: 92%. Color accuracy: Delta-E < 1.5. Brightness uniformity: > 95%. All panels tested per Buyer's Display Quality Specification DQS-2024-Rev3. Annual audit rights with 21-day notice.

ARTICLE 8 — SUSTAINABILITY
Supplier commits to RE100 renewable energy targets for panel manufacturing by 2026. Conflict mineral reporting per OECD guidelines. Annual sustainability audit results shared with Buyer.
