MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT — SHENTECH ACTUATOR SYSTEMS (SUP-003)
Contract Reference: MSA-2024-CAM-003
Effective Date: March 1, 2024
Expiration Date: December 31, 2026
Contracting Party: Global Hardware Manufacturing Corp.

ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE OF SUPPLY
ShenTech Actuator Systems ("Supplier") shall manufacture and deliver Camera Actuator Modules including Optical Image Stabilization (OIS) actuators, Voice Coil Motor (VCM) actuators, and Auto-Focus actuator assemblies for smartphone camera systems.

ARTICLE 2 — MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY
Minimum order quantity is 3,000 units per purchase order. Quarterly minimum commitment: 30,000 units. Small-lot orders below MOQ are accepted on an exception basis with 25% surcharge and 45-day extended lead time.

ARTICLE 3 — SOURCING ALLOCATION
Supplier is allocated a maximum of 40% of total Camera Actuator Module procurement volume. Given Shenzhen manufacturing concentration risk, allocation reviews are triggered automatically when regional sourcing from any single geography exceeds 50% of category volume.

ARTICLE 4 — LEAD TIME AND DELIVERY
Standard lead time is 35 calendar days. Complex multi-axis OIS actuator assemblies require 42-day lead time. Supplier must provide weekly production status updates for orders exceeding 50,000 units.

ARTICLE 5 — HEADROOM CAPACITY
Supplier maintains 60,000 units of headroom capacity. Surge order acceptance is contingent on current production line utilization. During peak production periods (Q3-Q4), headroom capacity may be reduced to 40,000 units with 30-day advance notice.

ARTICLE 6 — FORCE MAJEURE
Standard force majeure provisions apply. Additionally, due to Shenzhen's exposure to regional disruption events (typhoons, government-mandated shutdowns, power rationing), Supplier maintains a documented Business Continuity Plan (BCP) that includes:
(a) secondary production facility activation within 14 days
(b) minimum 30-day raw material buffer inventory
(c) cross-trained workforce deployment plan
BCP testing is required annually, with results shared with Buyer.

ARTICLE 7 — REGIONAL RISK PROVISIONS
Given the concentration of Camera Actuator Module manufacturing in the Shenzhen-Dongguan corridor, both parties acknowledge elevated regional disruption risk. In the event of a regional disruption affecting Shenzhen manufacturing for more than 14 consecutive days:
(a) Supplier activates BCP within 48 hours
(b) Buyer may immediately activate alternate sourcing without penalty
(c) Supplier provides daily operational status reports
(d) Transition support to alternate suppliers is mandatory for 90 days post-disruption

ARTICLE 8 — QUALITY
Products must meet Buyer's camera module reliability specification (CMS-REL-2024). Environmental testing per JEDEC JESD22 standards. Actuator lifetime: minimum 200,000 cycles. Supplier must maintain cleanroom manufacturing (Class 1000 or better).

ARTICLE 9 — PRICING
Fixed pricing for initial 18-month period. Rare earth material cost adjustments permitted semi-annually with 60-day notice and third-party commodity price verification. Volume discount tiers apply per Exhibit D.
