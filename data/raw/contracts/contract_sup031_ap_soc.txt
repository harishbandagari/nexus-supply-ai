MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT — HSINCHU APPLICATION PROCESSOR FOUNDRY (SUP-031)
Contract Reference: MSA-2025-SOC-031
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Expiration Date: December 31, 2028
Contracting Party: Premium Mobile Devices Corp.

ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE OF SUPPLY
Supplier shall provide leading-edge wafer foundry services for Buyer's custom mobile Application Processor System-on-Chip (AP SoC) on advanced N3 and N2 process nodes. Deliverables include wafer fabrication, advanced packaging (CoWoS and InFO), and known-good-die testing per specifications in Exhibit A.

ARTICLE 2 — MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY
Buyer commits to a minimum wafer start quantity of 500 wafers per purchase order. Annual wafer commitment shall not fall below 12,000 wafers.

ARTICLE 3 — SOURCING ALLOCATION
Supplier is approved for a maximum sourcing allocation of 45% of Buyer's total flagship AP SoC wafer demand. This concentration is permitted only because leading-edge foundry capacity at N3 and below is structurally constrained to a small number of qualified suppliers globally. Buyer shall maintain a documented dual-foundry qualification roadmap.

ARTICLE 4 — LEAD TIME AND DELIVERY
Standard lead time is 56 calendar days from wafer start. Supplier guarantees 97% on-time delivery rate measured quarterly.

ARTICLE 5 — HEADROOM CAPACITY
Supplier commits to maintaining 40,000 wafer-equivalent units of headroom capacity available for surge orders.

ARTICLE 6 — FORCE MAJEURE AND GEOPOLITICAL RISK
Both parties acknowledge elevated geopolitical exposure given supplier fab concentration in Hsinchu, Taiwan. Supplier shall maintain documented business continuity plans including secondary fab qualification at non-Taiwan facilities and a strategic finished-goods buffer of not less than 21 days. Geopolitical disruption exceeding 30 consecutive days entitles Buyer to invoke dual-foundry contingency without penalty.

ARTICLE 7 — QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE
All deliverables must conform to Buyer's PDK and design rule specifications. Wafer yield commitment: 90% functional die per wafer at maturity.

ARTICLE 8 — SLA ESCALATION
Standard three-tier escalation matrix applies.

ARTICLE 9 — PRICING AND PAYMENT
Wafer pricing fixed for 12 months. Annual price adjustments capped at CPI + 3%. Payment terms: Net 60 days from wafer-out.

ARTICLE 10 — TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 270 days written notice given long foundry transition cycles.
