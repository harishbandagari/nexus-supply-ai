MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT — PYEONGTAEK MOBILE DRAM (SUP-037)
Contract Reference: MSA-2025-LPDDR5-037
Effective Date: March 1, 2025
Expiration Date: February 28, 2028
Contracting Party: Premium Mobile Devices Corp.

ARTICLE 1 — SCOPE OF SUPPLY
Supplier shall manufacture and deliver LPDDR5 and LPDDR5X mobile DRAM packages (8GB, 12GB, and 16GB density options) for integration into flagship smartphone application processor stacks. Deliverables include known-good-die PoP (package-on-package) and discrete BGA configurations per Exhibit A.

ARTICLE 2 — MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY
Buyer commits to a minimum order quantity of 5,000 units per purchase order. Quarterly volume commitments shall not fall below 250,000 units.

ARTICLE 3 — SOURCING ALLOCATION
Supplier is approved for a maximum sourcing allocation of 40% of Buyer's total LPDDR5 mobile DRAM procurement volume. Buyer shall maintain at least two additional qualified LPDDR5 suppliers at all times to ensure dual-source resilience.

ARTICLE 4 — LEAD TIME AND DELIVERY
Standard lead time is 28 calendar days from purchase order acceptance. Supplier guarantees 95% on-time delivery rate measured quarterly.

ARTICLE 5 — HEADROOM CAPACITY
Supplier commits to maintaining 140,000 units of headroom capacity available for surge orders during NPI launch windows.

ARTICLE 6 — FORCE MAJEURE
Standard force majeure provisions apply. Memory market price volatility shall not constitute force majeure.

ARTICLE 7 — QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE
All deliverables must conform to JEDEC LPDDR5/LPDDR5X specifications. Defect rate must not exceed 30 PPM. Supplier shall maintain ISO 9001:2015 certification.

ARTICLE 8 — SLA ESCALATION
Standard three-tier escalation matrix applies.

ARTICLE 9 — PRICING AND PAYMENT
Pricing is reviewed quarterly and benchmarked against DRAMeXchange spot index. Payment terms: Net 45 days from invoice date.

ARTICLE 10 — TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 180 days written notice.
